About

I am a Senior Project Executive Officer in the Department of Electrical Engineering at IIT Kanpur, where I submitted my PhD thesis in January 2026. My doctoral research focused on next-generation blockchain architecture — spanning economic mechanisms, scalable infrastructure, and real-world deployment scenarios.

Currently, I lead the development of a Data Communication System (DCS) for Communication-Based Train Control (CBTC) for HBL Engineering, covering wireless networking, IEEE 1588 PTP time-synchronisation, and high-speed railway communications protocols.

As Co-Principal Investigator on a Department of Telecommunications (DoT) funded 6G project, my focus lies on real-time Channel State Information prediction using deep learning to reduce feedback overhead in next-generation networks.

Beyond research, I am passionate about mentoring the next generation of engineers and am actively seeking academic positions to bring my research and teaching experience to students.
